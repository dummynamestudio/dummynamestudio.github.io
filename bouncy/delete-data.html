Bouncy - Delete your data

Bouncy by DummyName Studio

Bouncy has no accounts: we never ask for your name or email address. The data about your play is either kept only on your phone or held by Google services the game uses (see the privacy policy). Here is how to delete each part.

How to request deletion

  1. Send us an email at dummynamestudio@gmail.com with the subject "Bouncy - delete my data".
  2. Say roughly when you played and on which phone model. We will reply within 30 days, tell you what we found, and delete the analytics data that can be linked to your device.
  3. On your phone, you can delete the rest yourself at any time:
    • uninstall Bouncy, or open Settings > Apps > Bouncy > Storage > Clear data - this deletes your settings, high scores and achievement progress at once;
    • reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings > Privacy > Ads (on most Android phones) - this unlinks the ad data collected so far from your phone;
    • if you use Google Play Games, delete Bouncy's achievements and leaderboard scores in the Google Play Games app (Profile > Settings > Delete Play Games account & data, where you can delete the data of a single game).
