Bouncy - Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

This privacy policy explains what information the mobile game Bouncy (the "Game") collects, why, and what choices you have. The Game is published by DummyName Studio ("we", "us"), an individual developer. If you have any question about this policy or your data, write to dummynamestudio@gmail.com.

In short: we do not ask for your name, email address or any account of ours, and we do not sell personal information. The Game uses services of Google to show ads, to understand how the Game is played and to offer Google Play Games achievements. These services collect some device information, as described below.

1. Information stored only on your device

The Game keeps the following on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else:

  • your settings (sound, music, vibration, language);
  • the high score tables, including the names you type in for them;
  • your achievement progress and how many games you have played (used to decide when ads may be shown).

Uninstalling the Game, or clearing its data in your device settings, deletes all of it.

2. Information collected by third-party services

The Game uses the following services provided by Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited. Each of them processes data as described in its own privacy policy, and we can only see this data in aggregated reports.

Google AdMob (advertising)

The Game is free and shows ads: a banner in the main menu and short full-screen video ads before some games. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and your interactions with ads. Where the law requires your consent (in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the Game asks for it with Google's consent form before any ad is requested. Everywhere, you can opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (see "Your choices"). Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences with parental guidance (PG).

Google Analytics for Firebase (game statistics)

To understand how the Game is played and to improve it, we use Google Analytics for Firebase. It collects pseudonymous usage data: which screens are opened; when a game starts and ends; the levels reached, scores, playing time and items picked up; achievements unlocked; ads shown; the language of the Game. It also collects technical data such as an app instance ID, the advertising ID, the device model, the operating system version, the app version and your approximate location (country or region, derived from your IP address). This data does not include your name, your email address or the names you enter in the high score tables, and we do not use it to identify you.

Google Play Games Services (achievements)

If you use Google Play Games, the Game sends the achievements you unlock and your progress toward them to Google Play Games, linked to your Play Games profile. Google decides how this is shown, according to your Play Games settings. The Game does not receive your email address.

Google Play

Google Play may provide us with aggregated statistics and crash and performance reports about the Game.

3. Why the data is used

  • to show ads, which pay for the free Game, and to measure them (legal basis in the EEA and the UK: your consent where it is required, otherwise our legitimate interest in funding the Game);
  • to understand how the Game is played, find problems and improve it (legitimate interest);
  • to offer Google Play Games achievements (performance of the service you chose to use).

4. Your choices

  • Ad consent: where the consent form applies, you can change your choice at any time with Privacy options in the Game's Settings.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it, and opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads on most Android devices).
  • Play Games: you can manage or delete your Play Games profile in the Google Play Games app.
  • Deletion and other requests: the steps are on the page Delete your data. You can uninstall the Game at any time. To ask for access to, or deletion of, data collected about your use of the Game, write to dummynamestudio@gmail.com. As we do not know who you are, we may need information from your device (such as the app instance ID) to find the data.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA and the UK, or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to it, and to complain to your data protection authority. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for purposes other than those above.

5. Retention and transfers

Analytics events are kept for 2 months. Data linked to your device in Google Analytics (such as the app instance ID) is deleted automatically 14 months after you last play. Data held by Google is kept according to Google's policies. Google processes data on servers in several countries, including the United States, using safeguards recognised by law, such as standard contractual clauses.

6. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, contact us and we will help to delete it.

7. Security

Data sent by the Game to Google's services is transmitted over encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we only work with services that use industry-standard protection.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
